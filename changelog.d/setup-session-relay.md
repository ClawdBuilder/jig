### Added

- `jig setup` adds a session relay, so a person with no company plugin can use Jira and logs through one: `jig setup transports` asks its name (letters, digits, `-` and `_`, up to 40), its address and where its token is kept, looks once at `GET /v1/health` without the token and says what it found. Jira's and logs' sign-in then offers "Through a session relay or transport" as a pick of the transports that exist (the person's, a company's defaults, or one added earlier in the same run), and writes `jira.auth.transport` or `logs.backend.transport`, then asks one optional time limit for a request (`jira.timeoutSeconds` or `logs.timeoutSeconds`, 5 to 120) and says what Enter keeps, since a browser relay can be slow right after you sign in (a Jira search keeps its own `searchTimeoutSeconds`); with none, it is not offered, and a company's own sign-in service stays company-only. A method names `viaTransport: true` for this, and an `object` item may now name the section itself as its map (empty `path`), write a form's `fixed` values without a question, and be `optional`; a method field may be a `number` (with a `default`) or carry a whole `question`, and a `reference` in an object may say `fileExpected`, so the relay's token lists a file first without the weak-choice warning and asks one line to turn files on when they are not a place Jig reads from yet.
