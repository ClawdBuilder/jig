### Added

- `jig setup` adds a session relay, so a person with no company plugin can use Jira and logs through one: `jig setup transports` asks its name, its address and where its token is kept, looks once at `GET /v1/health` without the token and says what it found. Jira's and logs' sign-in then offers "Through a session relay or transport" as a pick of the transports that exist (the person's, a company's defaults, or one added earlier in the same run), and writes `jira.auth.transport` or `logs.backend.transport`; with none, it is not offered, and a company's own sign-in service stays company-only. A method names `viaTransport: true` for this, and an `object` item may now name the section itself as its map (empty `path`), write a form's `fixed` values without a question, and be `optional`.
